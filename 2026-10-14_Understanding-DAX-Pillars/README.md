# Understanding DAX Pillars

**Speaker:** Francesco Bergamaschi  
**Termin:** 14.10.2026, 10:00–17:00 Uhr  
**Raum:** The Grid  
**Format:** Full Day Workshop · Introductory and overview

## Beschreibung

DAX is a simple language (but not easy at all). Its pillars, though, need to be clearly understood in order not to get confused when they interact between them in your measures, calculated columns or calculated tables. In this session the five DAX pillars (filter context, row context, context transition, iterations and expanded tables) will be explained, with  examples,  one by one first and then in interaction between them. Examples will be in increasing complexity order.
